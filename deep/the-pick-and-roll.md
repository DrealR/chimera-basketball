# The Pick and Roll

The best play in basketball is two people and one screen.

---

## How It Works

One player has the ball. Another player sets a screen. The ball handler drives off the screen. Now the defense has a problem.

```
IF THE DEFENDER GOES OVER THE SCREEN:
  The ball handler has a half-step.
  Drive to the rim. Pull up for the mid-range.
  The screener rolls to the basket for a lob.

IF THE DEFENDER GOES UNDER THE SCREEN:
  The ball handler has space.
  Pull up for the three. Or drive the open lane.
  The defense gave you the shot. Take it.

IF THEY SWITCH:
  Now there's a mismatch.
  A big guarding a guard, or a guard on a big.
  Attack the mismatch. Every time.
```

One screen. Three defensive responses. Three different attacks. That's why the pick-and-roll has been the best play in basketball for fifty years. It forces the defense to choose, and every choice gives the offense something.

---

## The Read

The play isn't in the screen. The play is in the READ.

The ball handler has to see what the defense does IN REAL TIME. Not before. Not after. In the half-second where the defender decides, the ball handler decides too. Two decisions happening simultaneously. The faster reader wins.

```
BAD PICK AND ROLL:
  Ball handler decides BEFORE the screen.
  "I'm going to drive left no matter what."
  The defense doesn't matter. The play is scripted.
  Easy to stop.

GOOD PICK AND ROLL:
  Ball handler decides DURING the screen.
  "What is the defense giving me RIGHT NOW?"
  The defense matters. The play is a conversation.
  Almost impossible to stop consistently.
```

That's why some duos are unstoppable and others are predictable. It's not the screen. It's the read.

---

## The Roller

The screener's job doesn't end at the screen. After contact, they roll to the basket. Hard. Fast. With their hands ready.

The roll is the second half of the conversation. The ball handler reads the defense. If the defense collapses on the ball handler, the roller is open. If the defense stays home, the ball handler finishes.

```
THE ROLL:
  Set the screen. Feel the contact.
  The defender went OVER. The ball handler is driving.
  ROLL HARD to the basket.
  Seal your man. Show your hands.
  The ball is coming.

THE POP:
  Set the screen. Feel the contact.
  The defense is sagging. Nobody's closing out.
  POP to the three-point line.
  The ball handler finds you. Wide open.
  Shoot it.
```

Roll or pop. The screener reads the defense too. Two readers, one screen, the defense can't guard both.

---

## Why It's Unstoppable

The pick-and-roll creates two-on-one situations out of five-on-five basketball. For one second, two offensive players are attacking one defensive problem. That's an advantage. The defense can recover, but recovery takes time. In that time, the offense has already made the play.

The best pick-and-roll teams don't just run it once. They run it until the defense breaks. Pick and roll. Defense recovers. Pick and roll again. Defense scrambles. Pick and roll a third time. Now the help defense is a step slow. Now the corner three is open. Now the weak side has a cutter.

The pick-and-roll isn't one play. It's a pressure test. Run it enough times and the defense cracks somewhere.

---

## Court Vision

The best point guards don't just read the screen. They read EVERYTHING.

They see the defender going under the screen. They see the help defender cheating off the corner. They see the weak-side big stepping up to protect the rim. They see the roller and the pop man and the corner shooter all at once.

Not because they have superhuman eyes. Because they've seen it ten thousand times. The patterns are stored. The reads are automatic. They don't think. They see.

Watch a great point guard in the pick-and-roll. Their head doesn't move. Their eyes see the whole floor. They feel where everyone is without looking. Coaches call it court vision. It's more than vision. It's knowing where your teammate is going BEFORE they go there.

---

*Two people. One screen. The simplest play in basketball and the hardest to stop. The pick-and-roll is a conversation between offense and defense. The faster reader wins.*
