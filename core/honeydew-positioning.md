# Positioning Over Chasing

The best defenders in basketball don't chase the ball. They're already where the ball is going.

---

## The Aphid on the Stem

There's a tiny insect called an aphid. It doesn't chase food. It positions itself on a plant stem where the sap flows, and the PRESSURE of the plant pushes the food to it. It keeps about 5% of what flows through. The rest it gives away — and that generosity attracts ants who protect it from predators.

The aphid doesn't hustle. The aphid positions.

Now watch the best players on a basketball court.

---

## Off-Ball Movement

Most of basketball happens without the ball. Five players on the floor, one ball. That means four players are moving without it at all times. Where they move — and WHY — decides everything.

```
THE CHASER:
  Runs toward the ball.
  Clogs the lane.
  Gets in the way of the ball handler.
  Creates nothing.

THE POSITIONER:
  Reads the defense.
  Moves to the open space.
  Arrives at the right spot at the right time.
  The ball finds them because they're where it needs to be.
```

You don't chase the ball in basketball. You position yourself where the ball WANTS to go. Then the game's natural pressure — the defense collapsing, the ball handler driving — pushes the ball to you. You didn't force anything. You were just in the right place.

---

## Defensive Positioning

Great defenders don't react to the offense. They take away options before the offense knows what it wants.

```
CHASING DEFENSE:
  The ball handler drives. You follow.
  They cross over. You're a step behind.
  They stop. You're still moving.
  They shoot. You can't contest.
  You were always REACTING.

POSITIONAL DEFENSE:
  You know where they want to go.
  You're already in that lane.
  They drive, but the lane is gone.
  They pull back. You haven't moved.
  They had to go to their second option.
  You controlled the play without touching the ball.
```

The best defensive play is the one the offense never tries. Because you were already there.

---

## Floor Balance

After a shot goes up, five players need to be in five specific zones. Offensive rebounders crash the glass. The safety gets back to prevent the fast break. The balance man reads what happens.

This isn't about effort. It's about structure. Five players in the right five spots means the team can rebound AND get back on defense. Five players all crashing the glass means one long pass beats all of them.

Keep 5% back. Send 95% forward. The 5% protects everything.

---

## The Lesson

Position yourself where the game flows. Don't chase. Don't force. Read where things are going and BE THERE. The game rewards the player who's in the right spot more than the player with the most effort in the wrong spot.

Hard work in the wrong position is just running. Smart positioning in the right spot is basketball.
