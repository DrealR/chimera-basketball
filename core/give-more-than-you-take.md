# Give More Than You Take

The best player on the court isn't always the one scoring.

---

## Assists Over Points

Watch the box score after any championship game. Look at the assists. Look at the hockey assists — the pass before the pass before the score. Look at the screens set, the lanes created, the defensive rotations that went unnoticed.

The guy who sets the screen that frees the shooter who passes to the cutter? HE started the play. Nobody in the crowd knows. Nobody tweets about it. The play doesn't exist without him.

That's the best kind of basketball. Invisible. Essential.

```
THE SCORER:
  Everyone sees it. Highlight reel. Stats column.
  Gets the glory.

THE CREATOR:
  Nobody sees it. No highlight. Maybe an assist.
  Made the glory possible.
```

The scorer takes from the game. The creator gives to the game. Both matter. But teams built around creators last. Teams built around scorers don't.

---

## The Pass-First Player

There's a moment in every possession where someone has a choice. I can shoot, or I can find the better shot.

The pass-first player asks one question: who's more open than me?

If the answer is someone else, the ball moves. Every time. No hesitation. No ego. The ball finds the best shot, not the best scorer. That's not selflessness — that's intelligence. The best shot wins more games than the best player taking bad shots.

```
A GOOD OFFENSE:
  The ball moves. Everyone touches it.
  Each pass creates a better angle.
  The defense breaks because they can't guard movement.
  EVERYONE is a threat because ANYONE might shoot.

A BAD OFFENSE:
  One guy holds the ball. Everyone watches.
  The defense loads up on one person.
  Four players standing. One player forcing.
  NOBODY is a threat because ONLY ONE might shoot.
```

---

## Pause Before You Decide

The worst turnovers happen in a rush. Caught the ball, panicked, threw it somewhere. No pause. No read. Just reaction.

The best players slow the game down. Not their feet — their mind. They catch the ball, and there's a moment where everything is quiet. Triple threat. Jab step. Head up. What's the defense doing? What are they giving me?

That half-second pause is the difference between a turnover and an assist.

It's not hesitation. Hesitation is fear. The pause is awareness. They look the same from the outside. They feel completely different from inside.

---

## Don't Force

The defense is talking to you. Every possession, they're telling you what's available. If they pack the paint, the three is open. If they switch everything, the mismatch is there. If they trap the pick-and-roll, the short roll man is free.

Bad teams force THEIR play against the defense.

Good teams read what the defense is GIVING them.

The best plays in basketball aren't plays at all. They're responses. The defense does something, and the offense answers. The defense adjusts, and the offense answers again. It's a conversation. You can't have a conversation if you're only talking.

Stop forcing the ball into the post when they're double-teaming. Stop pulling up for three when they're giving you the lane. Stop running the same play when it's been stopped three times.

Listen. The game is talking. What's it saying?

---

*The best player makes everyone else better. That's not a cliche. That's the deepest thing in basketball.*
