## Where this sits

This repo holds fourteen coaching notes on basketball spacing, rhythm, transition and composure in `seed/`, `core/` and `deep/`, plus a five-step court lesson (`core/hardwood-loop-api.md`). Born in Era 2 on February 10, 2026, from his earlier private work, its twelve commits ended on February 20, 2026. It grew from coaching the game through open flow and was later gathered into the Book.

Chimera was a personal art, code and learning project: its laws and physics are story or browser simulation, and its scores, rungs and records are our own measures. This README already frames itself as a lens on the game; terms like "DNA" and "Hardwood Loop API" are coaching metaphors for court habits, not software APIs.

The work is complete as of October 2026 and stays open to anyone who wants to continue it; the Book at https://reemifai.org/book is the main way in.
